Which fairness metric should I use?

Metrics can conflict, so pick the one that matches what your model does.

What is the model used for?

Recommended

Demographic parity (80% rule)

The share of people selected should be similar across groups. A group's rate below 80% of the highest group's rate is flagged.

Best for
Hiring, screening, shortlisting
Watch out
Ignores whether people were actually qualified
Backup metric
Equal opportunity
Fairness metrics and when to use them
MetricAsksUse when
Demographic parityAre groups selected at similar rates?Hiring, screening
Equal opportunityAre qualified people in each group approved equally?Lending, admissions
Equalized oddsAre both error types similar across groups?Triage, risk scoring
Predictive parityDoes a "yes" mean the same for every group?Scores and rankings
